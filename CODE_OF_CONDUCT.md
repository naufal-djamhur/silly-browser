# Community guidelines

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Technical disagreement is welcome; harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks and disclosure of another person's private information are not.

Keep discussions relevant, explain conclusions with evidence, and give others room to ask questions or correct mistakes. Do not post credentials, private browser profiles or personal captures.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and protect participants. Contact the repository owner through GitHub's available private contact/reporting tools for sensitive conduct concerns; do not disclose private details in a public issue.
